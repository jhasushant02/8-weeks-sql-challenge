# Results

Add a screenshot of each result grid to this folder, named after the question (`a02.png` ... `d04.png`).
The queries are in [`analysis/solutions.sql`](../analysis/solutions.sql); the walkthrough with outputs is in the blog post linked from the main README.

| Set | Questions | What the output answers |
|---|---|---|
| A. Data exploration and cleansing | A1 to A7 | rows per month, rows with no month, whether the two tables agree, the right join, date mismatches |
| B. Interest analysis | B1 to B5 | interests present in all 14 months, where the cumulative % passes 90%, what the 6-month cutoff removes, interests per month after filtering |
| C. Segment analysis | C1 to C5 | peak composition (top and bottom 10), lowest average ranking, most volatile interests and their min/max months |
| D. Index analysis | D1 to D5 | monthly top 10 by average composition, most frequent leader, average of the top 10, 3-month rolling average of the leader |

A1 (the date conversion) and A7 (the date check) return no result grid worth a screenshot beyond the confirmation counts; D5 is a written answer.
