1 in 4

Only about a quarter of visits see an ad, in every campaign. The ads are linked to much larger and more successful visits, so the clearest opportunity is to show them to more shoppers, and to get more of those viewers to click.

Read with care. These are associations, not proof of cause: shoppers who browse more both see more ads and buy more. Visits outside any campaign window purchased at 52.4%, higher than any campaign, so check the campaign dates. The BOGOF exposed group is only 74 visits. A holdout test would show what the ads cause.
